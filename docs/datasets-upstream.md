# Upstream Datasets

These are the **external** benchmarks and corpora MemGym wraps or draws on — as
distinct from the artifacts MemGym itself produces (those are in
[data.md](data.md)).

The canonical, in-package list with paper titles and source links is maintained
at [`../src/memgym/docs/datasets.md`](../src/memgym/docs/datasets.md). In brief:

- **Software engineering:** SWE-bench (+ Lite / Verified), SWE-Gym, SWE-smith.
- **Computer use / GUI:** WebArena-Infinity (and OSWorld, referenced in design).
- **Multi-hop QA (feed the MemGym-DR pipeline):** 2WikiMultihopQA, MuSiQue,
  HotpotQA.
- **Secondary / noise sources for IR:** Natural Questions, TriviaQA, CRAG, FRAMES.
